About our church

Who we are

Our story

The Full Gospel Church of God has its foundation of faith and practice in Scriptures, and the vital experience of its constituents is scripturally oriented.

  1. 1886

    The Church of God International is founded upon the principles of Christ as they are revealed in the Bible, the Word of God.

  2. 1951

    The Full Gospel Church of God in South Africa, its doctrine and memorandum of agreement, is confirmed in the amalgamation with the Church of God in the United States of America.

Our mission is to bring people to Jesus and membership in His family.

What we believe

The Bible is our foundation

Our faith and practice are rooted in the Scriptures, the Word of God.

Christ at the centre

We hold to the principles of Christ as they are revealed in the Bible.

Faith that is lived

The everyday experience of our members is scripturally oriented.

Our mission in three steps

  1. BringPeople to Jesus and into membership in His family.
  2. DevelopEach person to Christlike maturity.
  3. EquipThem for ministry in the church and their life mission in the world.

What to expect

When you visit us, you can expect a warm family atmosphere, uplifting worship, and practical, life-changing teaching straight from the Word of God. Come as you are, everyone is welcome!
